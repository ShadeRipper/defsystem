The defsystem plugin answers this command by opening the defsys side panel, so you should not be reading this.
If you are, the panel did not open. Call the mcp__defsystem__status tool, tell the person in two lines where the design
system stands, and say the panel needs a terminal at least 110 columns wide or a plugin reload.
